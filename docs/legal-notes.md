# Legal notes

[Türkçe](legal-notes.tr.md)

**These notes and the bundled templates are NOT legal advice.** OpenShelf provides editable text fields (Admin → Settings → Legal) for KVKK, privacy, cookies, distance sales and returns. Templates are starting points; laws, regulator guidance and your business facts differ. Have texts reviewed by a qualified lawyer before taking orders.

## Areas you must address (Turkey-focused)

- **KVKK (Law 6698)**: aydınlatma metni (privacy notice) at the points where personal data is collected; legal basis per purpose; retention periods; data subject request process; VERBIS registration if you exceed the thresholds; cross-border transfer rules when you use services outside Turkey (Cloudflare, analytics, ad platforms). Explicit consent is required for marketing e-mail/SMS under the relevant rules (ETK).
- **Cookies / consent**: non-essential cookies and trackers (analytics, marketing) need prior consent; the banner must allow equal-weight accept/reject. OpenShelf supports Consent Mode v2 and consent records; wording is yours ([tracking.md](tracking.md)).
- **Mesafeli satış (distance sales)**: pre-information form (ön bilgilendirme) and distance sales contract shown and accepted before payment (`acceptedTerms`), delivery within the statutory period, 14-day withdrawal right (cayma hakkı) with listed exceptions, withdrawal form, refund timelines.
- **Invoicing**: e-Fatura/e-Arşiv obligations depend on your taxpayer status. See [integrations/efaturam.md](integrations/efaturam.md).
- **ETBİS** registration for e-commerce businesses where applicable; commercial communication rules; marketplace (Trendyol) terms.
- **Consumer rights**: price display (tax included), returns, warranty, complaint channels.
- Other jurisdictions (GDPR, UK, US states) have their own rules.

## What OpenShelf gives you

Editable legal text fields, consent records (`customer_consents`) with policy version, order-time consent snapshot, customer anonymization endpoint, audit logs, data export. Responsibility for the content and for compliance stays with the store operator.
